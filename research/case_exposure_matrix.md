## Key Findings

### 1. Two-Tier Evidence Structure
- Tier 1 (21 cases): Has TLD evidence + page image + 4-7 cues in B2
- Tier 2 (9 cases): No TLD, no image, 0-2 cues in B2
- **Confound**: All N-type, U-type, and 2/4 S-type cases are in Tier 2

### 2. Zero-Cue Cases in B2
- 7 cases have ZERO cues displayed in B2
- For these cases: B1 == B2 (no presentation difference)
- These cases cannot contribute to B1-vs-B2 distillation comparison
- If assigned to B2, they dilute the cue effect measurement

### 3. Conflict Case Reclassification
- Genuine cue conflicts: 2 (AMB-313, AMB-462)
- Fake conflicts (no cue displayed): 2 (AMB-032, AMB-064)
- AMB-032/064: "conflict" is conceptual (absence of boundary markers), not observable
- WCAR/COR can only be measured on 2 genuine conflict cases

### 4. Exposure Design
- Random shuffle splits 30 cases into 3 sets of 10
- No stratification by tier, type, or difficulty
- Simulation shows Tier-2 per condition: min=0, max=8, mean=3.0
- **Risk**: One condition may get 6+ evidence-poor cases while another gets 0-1
